For a positive operator $mI\preceq L\preceq MI$, normalize $\|v\|=1$ and write
$r_0=v+e$, $\kappa=\|e\|$. The symmetric rank-two form
$(r_0v^*+vr_0^*)/2$ has eigenvalues $(1\pm\sqrt{1+\kappa^2})/2$.

\]

\section{Normalized IID-only forecast}\label{app:forecast}
Let $p_j=\Pr(r=1\mid B=j)$, $m_{j,+}=\E[s\mid B=j,r=1]$ and
$m_{j,-}=\E[s\mid B=j,r=0]$. Let $M_{-j}$ be the sum of independent Bernoulli
variables with parameters $p_\ell$ for $\ell\ne j$. Its law is computed by polynomial
convolution. Define
\[
u_j^+=\E\!\left[\frac{K-1-M_{-j}}{K d_{1+M_{-j}}}\right],\qquad
u_j^-=\E\!\left[\frac{M_{-j}}{K d_{M_{-j}}}\right].
\]
Conditioning on the reward of the item in stratum $j$ yields
\[
g_{\rm strat,std}=\frac1K\sum_j
\left[p_ju_j^+m_{j,+}-(1-p_j)u_j^-m_{j,-}\right].
\]
The implementation checks this population formula against all $2^K$ reward
configurations through $K=8$. For empirical IID banks, virtual-group integration
is conditional on the stored empirical distributions; finite-sample uncertainty
is based on original IID observations, not virtual combinations.
