\section{Limitations}
The strongest practical claims remain untested. Finite counterexamples establish
possibility, not prevalence, and the block-operator proof uses classical geometry.
Universal natural compatibility does not guarantee ascent under AdamW or under an
arbitrary noncommuting optimizer. The standardized extension assumes binary rewards
and a specific score-point loss, not general clipped multi-epoch GRPO.

The reference decoder retains arbitrary-precision latent intervals but computes
token CDFs numerically. It records CDF discrepancy and rejects support loss; this
does not prove exact real-valued softmax marginals on all prefixes. GPU throughput
and numerical tolerances need checkpoint-specific validation. Empirical rewiring
changes covariance and within-group text structure; small-step, independent-bank
and common-noise controls are needed to support a mean-direction interpretation.

\section{Discussion}
Dependence determines a distribution of contrasts. The score-subspace condition
specifies when its effect can be understood as reward-independent natural
preconditioning. Count-preserving intervention and IID prediction provide an
operational route to testing the remaining identity-selection component. Whether
this component is substantial and consequential for pretrained policies is the
question reserved for the bounded empirical program.
