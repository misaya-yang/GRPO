\section{Validation and experimental protocol}
\paragraph{Completed finite-policy checks.}
Table~\ref{tab:exact} reports CPU enumeration reproduced by the repository.
For the count example, $p_i=1/4$, $r=(1,1,0,0)$ and $s=(5,-7,0,2)$.
Two symmetric pair laws have the same marginal and count profile
$(\Pr(N=0),\Pr(N=1),\Pr(N=2))=(1/8,3/4,1/8)$.
Their coverage is $7/8$, compared with IID's $3/4$, yet their mean updates have
opposite signs. A separate stratified example uses $s=(1,-4,3,0)$ and $r=(1,0,0,0)$;
coverage rises from $7/16$ to $1/2$ while the update reverses.
These are restricted finite policies, not evidence of prevalence in pretrained models.

\paragraph{Pretrained-model protocol (not yet run).}
The bounded pilot uses full-parameter Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct \citep{qwen2025half},
unrestricted responses, $K=4$, and IID/stratified/lattice sampling. It contains
48 prompts (16 discovery, 32 confirmation), divided equally between generated
four-number arithmetic and SVAMP \citep{patel2021svamp}, with 16 groups per
prompt/sampler and a 160-token cap. The main bank has 9,216 responses before
separate calibration/evaluation additions.
All samplers use the same model revision, verifier and prompt distribution.

The primary statistic uses a fixed update map (initially SGD) and independent
evaluation directional scores:
\begin{equation}
\delta_{\rm func}=\min_{a\ge0}
\frac{\|d_W-a d_C\|_{F_{\rm eval}}}{\|d_X\|_{F_{\rm eval}}},\qquad
\|d\|_{F_{\rm eval}}^2=\E[(s^Td)^2].
\end{equation}
Independent original groups, nested within prompts, determine uncertainty.
Sample norms require bank replication or cross-bank products; an unresolved
reference norm makes the statistic unidentifiable. IID rewiring is a negative control.

\paragraph{Prediction and consequences.}
Compare the frozen IID forecast with discovery-fitted positive-scalar and count-only
comparators on independent coupled banks. Apply W/C/X and fixed doses from the same
checkpoint. Choose KL step sizes on separate calibration data, include a half step,
and check directional finite differences by step halving. Measure verifier reward
using fresh IID generation or an independent, unclipped likelihood-ratio estimate,
reporting ESS, weight tails and paired uncertainty. Reference-answer likelihood alone
does not establish a reasoning consequence. Before confirmation, the protocol also
registers a small set of behavioral quantities---held-out verifier reward, the
probability of a valid final-answer format, and a prespecified partition of
arithmetic solution/failure types---whose changes are forecast from independent IID
evaluation banks.

\paragraph{Decision criteria.}
The pilot requires passing implementation controls, a simultaneous lower 95\%
confidence bound above 0.10 for a prespecified non-scalar mechanism effect,
at least 25\% held-out forecast-error improvement with supporting uncertainty,
and a confirmed predicted verifier-reward effect at least 10\% of the resolved
cross-baseline reference slope. The 16 GPU-hour cap is a planning envelope.
An unresolved pilot is not a positive result.

Only after these gates pass does the plan expand to
$\{\text{IID, stratified, lattice}\}\times\{\text{within, cross}\}$ RLOO with
three seeds and two independent groups of four in every arm. Common IID evaluation,
token counts and actual elapsed time accompany all learning curves. Online trajectories
diverge as policies evolve; the strict same-trajectory causal claim belongs to the
frozen/branched experiment.
