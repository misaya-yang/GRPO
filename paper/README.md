# Manuscript

`main.tex` is a substantive initial manuscript based on the user-provided dossier:
introduction, operator/score-subspace theory, stratified/lattice consequences,
count-preserving intervention, IID forecasts, CPU results, experiment protocol and proofs.
Pretrained-model results are explicitly pending. This is not a submission-ready empirical paper.

The bundled `.sty` and `.bst` files are unmodified official ICLR 2027 resources from
[the official style archive](https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip),
linked by the [author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines).
Main-text submission budget is nine pages; the initial draft is not padded to fill it.

Build: `make paper` (requires latexmk/TeX Live) or compile `main.tex` with Tectonic.
Bibliography currently cites the subset used in the draft; the dossier retains the complete
primary-source audit. Extend related work during the final author review rather than
adding unverified references now. AI assistance disclosure is included for author review.
